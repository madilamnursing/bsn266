How to pick the answer

  1. ABCs first — airway > breathing > circulation.
  2. Unstable > stable, acute > chronic, actual > "risk for."
  3. Assess before you act — unless the client is in danger; then act.
  4. "Immediate intervention" = the new, sudden or life-threatening finding, not the expected one.
  5. NGN steps: Recognize cues → Analyze → Prioritize → Generate solutions → Act → Evaluate.
